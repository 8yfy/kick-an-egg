## Behavior and presentation

New players and Settings replay now begin with a centered welcome panel: "Welcome to kick the egg!" and "Press to continue with the tutorial!". Continue explicitly starts guidance; Skip dismisses it. No objective, spotlight or world trail appears before that choice. The subsequent instructions follow real actions one at a time, with a numbered stage heading. Eggs and stored pets are selected through their actual hotbar slots before pen navigation; already held items go directly to the pen.

The server observes real sack hits, current kick/chase state, inventory, placed eggs/pets, owned pen and income. Existing accomplishments take priority. Early eggs go straight to their hotbar slot or the pen when already held; arriving inside the pen switches guidance to placement on the grass. Existing eggs and pets skip earlier lessons. There is no spawn detour.

The free starter sack already exists in SackGear/Hotbar.Slot0, so its real control is highlighted without a shop detour. Three real hits introduce training. Training and pen facts persist through profile serialization; respawn preserves training while resetting the character counter baseline. Replay from Settings observes current progress without resetting saved completion/skip, inventory or rewards. Local replay tracks replicated sack hits even if the server tutorial was skipped.

Main guidance ends at the first placed pet and a brief income introduction: at least two seconds with income observed, or a five-second explanation fallback if the income tick is delayed. Affordable pen/speed upgrades are optional dismissible tips. No purchase, reward, camera takeover or movement restriction is introduced.
